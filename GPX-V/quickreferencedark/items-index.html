GPX-V Main Window

  • Action Buttons perform available actions. Grouped by video, video and GPS, and GPS.
  • Add Trackpoint Button adds a trackpoint at the location on the map at the time specified in video
  • Delete Trackpoints Button deletes the selected trackpoints
  • Elevation Button Adjusts elevations and gradients
  • Elevation Chart shows route elevation and slope gradient. Click or ⇧-click to select trackpoints.
  • Export GPX Button exports a synchronized GPX file
  • Export Video Button exports a syncrhonized .mp4 file
  • GPX-V Document Window displays video, map, elevation chart, trackpoint table, and document contents.
  • Help Button opens this tutorial.
  • Import GPX Button imports a GPX file
  • Import Video Button imports one or more .mp4 video files
  • Information Option Control shows three classes of available information. Click the one you want to see.
  • Information View shows the document change history, checks for potential problems, and current project status.
  • Interval Control shows applied speed changes, filters, and titles. Click to edit.
  • Interval Control shows applied speed changes, filters, and titles. Click to edit.
  • Link Menu controls the direction of the link between video and trackpoints.
  • Map Type Control switches between traffic and satellite views.
  • Remove Audio Button deletes audio tracks
  • Route Interpolation Button interpolates a route between two selected points
  • Route Map displays your route.
  • Segment Delete Button deletes selected trackpoints and video
  • Selected Video Frames shows the first, middle, and last frames of the selection.
  • Selection Range Slider controls selected video and trackpoints. Slide, click or ⇧-click to change the selection. Use the buttons at each end to adjust the ends of the range by one second at a time.
  • Share Video Button shares a frame or clip
  • Speed Adjustment Button opens an editor to apply speed adjustments to video and GPX simultaneously.
  • Subscription Button shows your GPX-V subscription status and lets you subscribe, upgrade, or manage your subscription.
  • Synchronize Button aligns the video and trackpoints, optionally trimming durations.
  • Tick Backward Button moves the selection one second backward.
  • Tick Forward Button moves the selection one second backward.
  • Titles Button opens an editor to add text to the video for a selected segment
  • Trackpoint Edit Button Edits the selected trackpoints
  • TrackPoint Inference Checkbox controls whether GPX-V shows inferred trackpoints to fill in gaps where user or GPS trackpoints are missing.
  • Trackpoint Table lists the GPS trackpoints in order. Click a row to select the point, ⇧-click to select a range. Arrow keys work too.
  • Trim From Start Button trims from the start of the video until the first selected trackpoint
  • Trim Until End Button trims from the last selected trackpoint until the end of the video
  • Video Filter Button opens an editor to apply image filters to the selected video to compensate for shadows, glare, etc.
  • Video Player plays the imported and merged video files.
  • Video Workspace contains the video player and selection controls.
  • Zoom In Button zooms in as close as possible on the selected trackpoint.
  • Zoom Out Button zooms out to view the entire route.

Title Editor

  • Add Title Button adds a new title to the list.
  • Delete Title Button deletes the selected title.
  • Subtitle shows the subtitle that will be applied. Click to edit.
  • Text Placement Offset Control sets how far from the edge of the video frame the titles will appear.
  • Title shows the title that will be applied. Click to edit.
  • Title Apply Button saves any changes you made and quits the Title Editor.
  • Title Cancel button discards the changes you made and quits the Title Editor.
  • Title Editor inserts and formats text into segments of the video.
  • Title Font Controls set the font family, style, size and color that will be applied.
  • Title Frame Preview shows the first, middle, and last video frame to which the title will be applied.
  • Title Preview button opens a window that plays a preview of the selected title.
  • Title Range Label shows the start, finish and length of the video segment to which the title will be applied.
  • Title Selection Range Slider selects the time range in the video to which the selected title will be applied. Slide, click or ⇧-click to change the selection. Use the buttons at each end to adjust the ends of the range by one second at a time.
  • Title Table View lists the titles you have applied to the video.
  • Title Text Placement Control positions the title text in the video frame. Click to select the desired position.

Video Filter Editor

  • Color Temperature Slider adjusts the color temperature of the applied filter.
  • Color Tint Slider adjusts the tint of the applied filter.
  • Gamma Slider adjusts the gamma (mid-tone lift) of the applied filter.
  • Highlight Protection Slider adjusts the highlight protection of the applied filter.
  • Shadow Lift Slider adjusts the shadow lift of the applied filter.
  • Video Filter Add Button adds a new filter to the list.
  • Video Filter Apply button applies the changes you made and quits the Video Filter Editor.
  • Video Filter Cancel button discards the changes you made and quits the Video Filter Editor.
  • Video Filter Delete Button deletes the selected filter.
  • Video Filter Editor defines and applies video filters to segments of the video.
  • Video Filter Original Frames shows the first, middle, and last video frames to which the filter will be applied.
  • Video Filter Preview button opens a window that plays a preview of the applied filter.
  • Video Filter Preview Frames shows the effect of the applied filter on the first, middle, and last video frame selected.
  • Video Filter Range Label shows the start, finish and length of the video segment to which the filter will be applied.
  • Video Filter Selection Range Slider selects the time range in the video to which the selected filter will be applied. Slide, click or ⇧-click to change the selection. Use the buttons at each end to adjust the ends of the range by one second at a time.
  • Video Filter Table View lists the filters you have applied to the video.

Interpolation Editor

  • Cancel Button closes this editor without making any changes.
  • Interpolation Apply Button inserts the interpolated trackpoints into your route.
  • Interpolation Elevation Chart show the elevation and gradients of the route, with selection.`
  • Interpolation Map shows the existing route, the selected segment, and the expected interpolation.
  • Interpolation Summary Label describes the trackpoints you have selected for interpolation.
  • Interpolation Time Range Label shows the video and trackpoint time interval you have selected.
  • Interpolation Type Control chooses between Bicycle, Automobile, Walking or Any routing using Apple Maps.
  • Trackpoint Interpolation Editor improves the route accuracy between two waypoints.

Speed Editor

  • Speed Adjustment Add Button adds a new speed adjustment to the list.
  • Speed Adjustment Apply/Confirm Changes button saves any changes you made and quits the Speed Adjustment Editor.
  • Speed Adjustment Cancel button discards the changes you made and quits the Speed Adjustment Editor.
  • Speed Adjustment Delete Button deletes the selected speed adjustment.
  • Speed Adjustment Direction Control sets whether the speed will be adjusted up or down.
  • Speed Adjustment Editor raises or lowers video and trackpoint speeds for selected segments.
  • Speed Adjustment Elevation Chart shows how your route changes elevation. Colors signal the gradient of the slope. Click to select a trackpoint, ⇧-click to select a range.
  • Speed Adjustment Factor Control sets the factor for speed adjustment. 2x down slows 120 fps video to 60 fps video, adjusting trackpoint times to match.
  • Speed Adjustment Frame Preview shows the first, middle, and last video frames to which the speed adjustment will be applied.
  • Speed Adjustment Range Label shows the start, finish and length of the video segment to which the speed adjustment will be applied.
  • Speed Adjustment Table View lists the speed adjustments you have applied.

GPX-V Library

  • GPX-V Library Window displays all the projects you have created, across devices and volume.
  • Library Summary displays the total number of your projects, source video storage used, and distance covered.
  • Machine Popup controls the devices from which you are displaying GPX-V projects in the Project Table.
  • Notes Label shows additional information as needed.
  • Open Button opens the selected project. You can also double-click the project in the table.
  • Project Table displays projects and their associated data items.
  • Refresh Button updates the list of projects.
  • Scan Button searches for projects in the folder you select.
  • Volume Popup controls the volumes from which you are displaying GPX-V projects in the Project Table.

Remove Selection Editor

  • Remove Selection Cancel Button closes the editor without making any changes.
  • Remove Selection Clip Summary describes your selected range.
  • Remove Selection Editor can remove the selected video and trackpoints.
  • Remove Selection Elevation Chart displays the elevation along the route and your selection.
  • Remove Selection Preview button plays a preview of what your video will look like after the selection is deleted.
  • Remove Selection Range Slider selects the time range in the video and trackpoint list which will be deleted.
  • Remove Selection Trim Button removes the selected video and trackpoints from your project.
  • Selected Range Label shows the duration of the selected video and trackpoints.
  • Video Frame Selection displays the first, middle, and last video frame selected.
